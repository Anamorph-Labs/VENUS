# "The Night Baker": an invented 5-line test script. One spoken line per row.
Every night at three, Mara unlocks the bakery on Juniper Street.
She starts the ovens before the city wakes up.
The first loaf is always for the bus driver on the early route.
Nobody asked her to do it, and she never says why.
But if you are up before dawn, follow the smell of bread.
