# "The Lost Umbrella": an invented 6-line test script. One spoken line per row.
Lost umbrellas have a secret life.
Most of them end up in a small office under the train station.
A man named Theo sorts them by colour, then by size.
Every Friday, he opens one at random and makes a wish.
He says it has worked twice.
So if you lose yours, it might be granting wishes now.
