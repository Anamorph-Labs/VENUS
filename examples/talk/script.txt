Your phone did not die because it was old.
It died because it was cold.

Inside every battery, tiny particles carry the charge from one side to the other.
In the cold, they slow to a crawl.
At zero degrees, a battery can lose about twenty percent of its power.
Below minus ten, it can lose almost half.

So keep your phone in an inside pocket, close to your body.
The power comes right back the moment it warms up.
