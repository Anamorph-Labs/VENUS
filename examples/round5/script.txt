# "The Night Market": an invented 5-line test script. Each line has two takes
# with the same words; one is weaker in delivery (quiet, rushed, trailing off, distorted).
The night market opens when the last train leaves.
Every stall sells something you did not know you needed.
My favourite is a man who repairs broken clocks for free.
He says time is the only thing he cannot fix.
So we visit him, and we lose track of it.
