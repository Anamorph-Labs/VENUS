# "The Paper Boats": an invented 5-line test script (line 5 is never recorded, on purpose). One spoken line per row.
Every spring, the river behind our school floods for one week.
The younger kids fold paper boats and race them to the bridge.
Nobody has ever seen a boat reach the bridge.
But every year, somebody folds one more.
If one ever makes it, we will finally know where the river goes.
