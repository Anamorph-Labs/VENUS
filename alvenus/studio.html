How the scene is built

  1. Each box is one part of the scene: the paper, the photo, the words. Click one to change it on the right.
  2. Lines show what goes where. Hover a line to read what it carries. Right-click it to add a tool, such as a blur or a glow.
  3. Or describe it in your words in the box at the bottom: “soft glow on the words”, “blur the paper”.
