How the scene is built

  1. Each box is one part of the scene: the paper, the photo, the words. Click one to change it on the right. Drag a box to move it.
  2. Lines show what goes where. Hover a line to read what it carries. Right-click it to add a tool, such as a blur or a glow.
  3. Or describe it in your words in the box at the bottom: “soft glow on the words”, “blur the paper”.

Venus is started by Anamorph Labs and grows with the people who use it. Have an idea? Ask your AI to suggest it (alvenus suggest). Anamorph Labs reviews every idea, builds the good ones, and thanks you by name.
